# Movie Industry Analysis

## 🎯 Objective
Identify key factors that influence movie success, focusing on revenue and ratings.

## 📊 Business Context
Movie studios invest millions in productions. Understanding what drives success can help optimize investment decisions.

## 🔍 Key Questions
- What factors influence revenue?
- Does budget correlate with success?
- Which genres perform best?
- Does release timing impact performance?

 ## 📈 Key Insights
- High budget does not guarantee higher revenue
- Certain genres consistently outperform others
- Revenue distribution is highly skewed, with a few blockbusters dominating

 ## 💡 Recommendations
- Focus investment on high-performing genres to increase the probability of success
- Avoid relying solely on high budgets, as they do not guarantee higher revenue
- Consider release timing strategically to maximize audience reach and revenue potential

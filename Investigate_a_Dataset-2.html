Introduction¶

Dataset Description¶

The TMDb movie dataset contains information about movies such as popularity, budget, revenue, vote average and release year.

This dataset will be used to explore patterns in movie ratings and financial performance.

The goal of this analysis is to undestand how movie ratings are distributed, which movies generated the highest revenue and whether there is a relationship between movie budget and revenue.

Key Questions¶

  1. How are movie ratings distributed in the dataset?
  2. Which movies generated the highest revenue?
  3. Is there a relationship between movie budget and revenue?
  4. How many movies are released per year?

Conclusions¶

In this project, the TMDb movie dataset was explored to understand movie ratings and box office revenue patterns.

The analysis showed that most movies have ratings between 5 and 7, suggesting that films generally receive moderately positive reviews. Very few movies receive extremely low or extremely high ratings.

The analysis also showed that the movies with the highest revenue are mostly large blockbuster productions such as Avatar, Star Wars and Titanic. The scatter plot suggests a positive relationship between budget and revenue, although the relationship is not perfectly linear.

One limitation of this analysis is that the dataset does not include other important factors that my influence movie success, such as marketing budgets or distribution strategies.

Further Research¶

Further research could explore how other variables such as genre, director, cast, and production companies influence movie revenue and ratings.

It would also be useful to include additional data such as marketing budget, streaming performance, awards, or audience demographics to better understand what drives movie success.

One limitation of this analysis is that it only shows relationships between variables. It does not prove that one variable causes another. More detailed data and further statistical analysis would be needed to investigate causation.
